Weapon Attachment Editor

Upload your own ships, then place firing nozzles and effect anchors visually. Drag markers to match your artwork.

01 / Ship Preview & Attachments

DRAG TO POSITION

Tap “Add Fire Point”, then drag the gold marker onto the exact gun nozzle. The arrow shows the bullet direction. Markers are stored relative to the image, so they stay aligned when the ship is resized.

02 / Custom Ship Asset

PNG · WEBP · JPG

Transparent PNG/WebP recommended. The preview uses “contain” fitting; your original image is not stretched. Uploading a ship does not automatically detect its nozzles—you set those precisely with the markers.

03 / Test Fire

Test Fire launches a preview beam from every configured Fire Point in its selected direction. It is a visual alignment test, not the complete game combat system.

Attachment Types
